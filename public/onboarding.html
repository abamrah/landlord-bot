Your AI property manager is ready. Here's a quick overview of what NestMind AI can do for you:

📱 AI Maintenance Triage

Tenants message your WhatsApp number. The AI classifies urgency (emergency / normal / cosmetic), drafts a reply, and logs a ticket in your dashboard — all automatically.

🤖 Smart Autopilot

Enable autopilot to auto-reply to low-severity messages. High-severity issues are held for your approval. Configure delay, cooldown, and message style.

💰 Utility Billing & Splits

Log utility bills per unit. For shared units, the AI calculates each tenant's share and sends personalized reminders via WhatsApp or to unit groups.

👥 WhatsApp Groups

Create WhatsApp groups for multi-occupant units. Send announcements, utility reminders, and welcome messages to the entire group. Group messages are routed to your AI too.

⏰ Automated Reminders

Schedule monthly rent and utility reminders. Choose friendly or formal tone. Messages go out on the day and time you pick, converted to each tenant's local timezone.

⚖️ Legal Compliance

AI responses are guided by your region's tenancy laws (RTA). It knows legal timeframes, tenant rights, and proper procedures for your jurisdiction.

💡 Quick Tips to Get Started

  • 1️⃣ Send a test message from your tenant's phone to verify the AI responds correctly.
  • 2️⃣ Go to AI Agent → Settings to customize the autopilot delay and message tone.
  • 3️⃣ Check the Tips & Guides section in your dashboard sidebar for step-by-step tutorials.
  • 4️⃣ Add your contractors so the AI can suggest assigning them to maintenance tickets.
  • 5️⃣ Set up utility credentials under Utilities to enable automatic bill fetching.
